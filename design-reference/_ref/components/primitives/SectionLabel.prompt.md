Section label with the redaction-mark motif — cream text whose `highlight` words sit on a solid 3px-radius block.

```jsx
<SectionLabel highlight="Electric Sheep?">Do Androids dream of</SectionLabel>
```

On cream sections the label line is near-invisible and the ink block reveals the highlight; on `[data-theme="dark"]` the block flips to cream and redacts it. This inversion is intentional brand behavior.
